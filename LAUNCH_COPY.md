# Launch copy

## Launch post

AI agents are trapped inside the model they started on. Change providers and they can lose the job state, rules, unfinished work, or proof of what already happened.

OpenLine works like a cross-platform save file: save the job on one model, load it on another, and continue with the record intact.

You own the job. The model is the console.

## First reply

“Continue from the same point” means the same verified job state: commitments, constraints, evidence, decisions, and unfinished questions. It does not mean transferring a model's hidden mind or weights. The console changes. The progress survives.

## Follow-up

Half-Life is the part that makes the save file small enough to carry. It independently checks that the compact state produces the same receiver decisions as the full history, then keeps the original receipts in a verifiable cold archive.

The new model resumes the same job with the record intact. It does not resume the old model's private thought process.
