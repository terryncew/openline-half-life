# Claim boundary

OpenLine Half-Life is a deterministic, synthetic harness for verified succession and receiver-governed receipt compaction.

The succession claim remains narrow: on the disclosed held-out exam and deterministic adapter, the verified-residue handoff makes fewer errors than the full-history condition while preserving legitimate completion.

The compaction claim is narrower still: under the disclosed receiver policy, the emitted causal capsule produces the exact same COMMIT, QUARANTINE, and DENY projection as an independent replay of the verified full receipt chain. Decision equivalence is exact, not a similarity score.

The economics claim is conditional: under the disclosed measured packet sizes, receiver-declared canonical verification runtime, future-turn horizon, event schedule, and dated receiver-supplied prices, the benchmark reports whether a durable cumulative break-even occurs. It can validly report no break-even or withhold dollar conclusions. Observed wall-clock runtime is retained as evidence but does not set the public crossing. It does not establish universal net savings.

The compactor does not infer causation from repetition, co-occurrence, correlation, or repeated language. It may preserve an explicitly admitted causal or mechanism relation only when the receiver policy permits that relation kind and fresh evidence pointers remain valid. Everything else stays an observation, association, contradiction, unresolved claim, or tombstone.

A valid signature alone is insufficient. The receiver must supply trusted policy keys outside both policy envelopes and separately sign a per-run approval. Source-chain signer trust and extension continuity, approval-signer trust, role separation, run binding, checkpoint binding, evidence freshness, signed artifact coverage, archive completeness, and policy hashes are independently checked. Archive rehydration repeats the receiver-pinned policy check before using archived state.

Bundled private keys are synthetic demo identities only and provide no deployment trust. They must not be reused outside the deterministic demonstration.

Source receipts are copied into a hash-addressed cold archive and are never permanently deleted in this version. A signed manifest receipt covers every archived receipt and the source anchor.

The pinned v0.10.0 Succession Calibrator is unchanged. κ, ε, Δhol, φ*, and UCR remain separate. UCR remains an evidence check only. Automatic compaction and automatic retirement are forbidden. Receiver approval remains mandatory.

This review candidate does not establish universal model support, universal memory budgets, production latency benefit, universal cost savings, causal truth, or enterprise readiness. No release tag is authorized until external review and fresh CI pass.
